Anosia Dragon

2019 . 1st Wave . Pre-Evolved . Invisibility

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Anosia Dragon!
The Anosia Dragons Celebrate their Pet Day on December 29th
You can claim a free Anosia Dragon on that day in our discord server!

The Anosia Dragons are a unique Petpet species that can only be found in the Theta region! They are a rare species and are only available during the festival! The Anosia dragons are fun-loving little tricksters.

They are often invisible to the naked eye and like to play tricks and steal things from the Floss. However, once you gain one's trust, they will be your companion for life. They love cuddles and playtime and knocking things off of shelves.

Anosia Kirin

2020 . 1st Wave . Evolved . Invisibility

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Anosia Kirins!
The Anosia Kirins Celebrate their Pet Day on February 17th
You can claim a free Anosia Kirin on that day in our discord server!

Anosia Kirin are majestic beasts who soar through the skies of Theta. These dragons thrive in the Theta wildlands and rule over the Anosia Dragons. Even the most excitable and Rascally Anosia Dragon can be stopped in its tracks by the roar of a Kirin. The Anosia Kirin are a proud species, and will not tolerate disrespect from a Floss, to tame one you must have a strong bond with an Anosia Dragon that carries on into their adulthood.

Anosia Kirin have the ability to become invisible and use it to protect their homes and families. If spotting an Anosia Dragon is rare, then seeing a Kirin in the wild is nearly impossible, however the lights and sounds of the Asylia Festival tend to draw them out. If youre lucky you might spot a wild Kirin frolicking through the skies with their baby Anosia Dragons.

Take a look! What's this! The Kirin holds a precious egg close to their bellies! During it's time as an Anosia Kirin, these dragons are starting their nests and will often have an egg they are incubating! The Kirin's are very protective of their eggs and rarely let it out of their sight! Sometimes even Domesticated Kirin will not allow their Floss companions to touch their eggs, but if it trusts you with all its heart it may allow you to hold onto the egg for them. For a limited time of course!

Ponneli

2021 . 2nd Wave . Pre-Evolved . Invisibility

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Ponnelis!
The Ponnelis Celebrate their Pet Day on August 21th
You can claim a free Ponneli on that day in our discord server!

Hop! Skip! Jump! The Ponnéli are wraskly wabbits that bound from tree to tree in the Theta forests! These furry bunnies are about the size of a house cat and have a unique skin pouch that they pull over their head like a hood. It's rare to see a Ponneli without its hood up in the wild, and even tamed Ponneli will be grumpy when you try and wash beneath their hoods. The Ponnéli are devious creatures who love to play pranks and steal food from unsuspecting floss.

Don't let their adorable eyes fool you! The Ponnéli are known to attack if they see you in their territory and are known to even duke it out against big petpets like the Charion and Paizo Beast! They can jump higher than you'd think and have razor sharp claws! Scared? Don't be! While wild Ponnéli can be dangerous the Theta know just the trick to get on one of these little bunnies' good sides! If you can tame one, they can be lifelong companions! Fluffy bellies and floppy ears aside the Ponnéli make great hunting companions for a Theta!

With their invisibility ability the Ponnéli stay close to their master's and can even carry supplementary weaponry for tight situations! The Ponnéli are a must have for Theta hunters and a must avoid for pretty much everyone else!

Ponnbuki

2022 . 2nd Wave . Evolved . Invisibility

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Ponnbukis!
The Ponnbukis Celebrate their Pet Day on January 19th
You can claim a free Ponnbuki on that day in our discord server!

The Ponnpuki call the forests of Theta their hunting grounds. These giant 3-foot-tall rabbits are adept hunters, leaping from trees and rooftops and making themselves nearly invisible in the leaves. The Ponnbuki are skilled and proud creatures, who don't take kindly to being kept as pets in the traditional sense, rather they prefer a friendship or professional relationship with the floss they choose to hunt with.

The Ponnbuki are silent when moving around and can often scare their floss companions without meaning to. They are big furry friends at the end of the day though, and if they're patient enough may even let you cuddle them on the couch.

Ponnbuki are able to be trained with weapons and can even be trained in swordsmanship, shooting arrows, and throwing knives. They are an invaluable asset in the forests of Theta but can also be dangerous if you encounter a rogue Ponnbuki in the wild so watch out!

Tama Tori

2023 . 3rd Wave . Un-Evolved . Invisibility

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Tama Toris!
The Tama Toris Celebrate their Pet Day on November 5th
You can claim a free Tama Tori on that day in our discord server!

The Tama Tori are small egg-shaped critters that love to roam around southern glykos and psekazi. They may appear like eggs, but their 'shell' is attached to their body and cannot be removed without killing them! (please dont try) The Tama Tori can come in a slight variation of sizes from as large as a baseball to about the diameter of a basket ball, but they dont tend to grow over time, just sticking to one size until they evolve. The Tama Tori can crack and open their shell at will, and then close it up again seamlessly. This shell-like covering is hard like a bone and cannot be easily broken, so dont worry!

The shell is not as hollow as it may seem, but when opened, the Tama Tori's body is pressed up against the crack but is always too dark in color to make out any detail. When touched (they hate when you do that) it is fuzzy. The Tama Tori are Timid creatures but when shown kindness bu a floss will gladly make itself comfortable in your home. They make great - albeit high maintenance - petpets. The Tama Tori can be little rascals when theyre comfortable, often stealing items, hiding under furniture, and knocking things off shelves. In the wild the Tama Tori will separate from their parents shortly after being laid and run far from their nests until they find a different petpet species' nest (commonly Anosia) to stay in.

They will blend in with the eggs around them and pretend to be one of this species' eggs. Sometimes Anosia Kirin can even be found carrying a Tama Tori around in place of it's own egg. These creatures have a tendency to find odd or obscure places to hide, so it is not uncommon for a Theta floss to stumble across one in the crook of a tree, in a gutter, or nestled between vending machines.

It is trying to hide, though it's bright colors and patterns often give it away. Children in Theta are delighted by finding these hidden eggs and will sometimes make a game of it. The Tama Tori are herbivores and are harmless to Candyfloss. They make great hunting for other carnivorous creatures though, which is why they try to make themselves a part of the family for stronger creatures. That includes Floss, so don't be surprised if one starts to follow you around!

Dai Tori

2024 . 3rd Wave . Evolved . Invisibility

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Dai Toris!
The Dai Toris Celebrate their Pet Day on September 19th
You can claim a free Dai Tori on that day in our discord server!

The Dai Tori are a good deal taller than an average Candyfloss and can weigh massive amounts. They are seldom seen outside of Theta, and prowl around the bamboo forests at nighttime. They are covered in fluffy fur and feathers and can tightly coil themselves into a ball to sleep in such a way that the shell remnants on their backs touch each other, making them appear like a very large egg. Despite their cute and fluffy exterior, the Dai Tori are fierce predators that will attack and kill floss if they find them roaming around in the Thetan forest.

Most floss stay far away from Dai Tori when they see one, but the Shinobi of Theta are trained on how to deal with one in the wild. The Dai Tori are slow moving and not too bright, so they are easy to outmaneuver and escape from if you know what you're doing. Some Shinobi train Dai Tori from the egg stage to use them in battle.

They are easily food motivated, so if you keep them well trained and feed them often, they won't pose a threat to Floss unless you command them to. It's rare to see two Dai Tori in one place at one time, however they are often accompanied by a gathering of Tama Tori who run around the Dai Tori's feet and gather underneath them when they sit down to hide under their abundant toosh fur. The Dai Tori call out in a distinct birds' caw that can often be heard from a distance all throughout Theta when the sun starts to set and the Dai Tori come out of their shells to hunt.
