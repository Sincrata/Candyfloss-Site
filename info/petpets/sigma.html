Glorbit

2023 . 1st Wave . Pre-Evolved . Electricity

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Glorbit!
The Glorbits Celebrate their Pet Day on October 6th
You can claim a free Glorbit on that day in our discord server!

The Glorbits are creatures native to the Sigma's moon Thymono! They lived alongside the Sigma in space and now call Caramella their home too! They are small squishy critters who are very abundant and float around in colorful flocks around Thymono! They don't exactly fly, more like float in the air like they're filled with helium! They turn slowly and glide into walls and things when they float about. They can steer themselves and push themselves forward by moving their tentacles but always have a floaty nonsense about their movement. Around them there is a ring or two of space dust that glows and spins around their bodies.



The Glorbits are very glowy, and can illuminate a room! The lights on their tentacles flicker and even change color seemingly at random! Glorbits are docile creatures and love the float about and cuddle their floss friends! The Sigma love their Glorbits and can't wait for the rest of Caramella to love them too! Glorbits like to nest in craters on Thymono so if you take one home be sure to give them a dark crevice to sleep in! The Glorbits produce electricity and can shock people on accident so be careful when you handle one! They even occasionally shock themselves if they gather too much static and can sometimes get stuck to carpets or blankets if they're having a bad static day so keep an eye on them! The Glorbits make a chirping sound that's rather cute and cheerful! Come see the Glorbits all over Thymono and take one home with you!

Glorbeast

2024 . 1st Wave . Evolved . Electricity

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Glorbeasts!
The Glorbeasts Celebrate their Pet Day on March 3rd
You can claim a free Glorbeast on that day in our discord server!

The majestic Glorbeast is a rare sight, with only 10 or 12 recorded to be living around Thymono at a time. The Glorbit pets very rarely evolve, and no one is quite sure what triggers it for them. The Glorbeast are slow moving and calm creatures, who can float in the air or on the surface of the sea surrounding their island home.

They are regarded by the native Sigmafloss as great beasts to be protected, cherished, and adored. If someone is unkind to a Glorbeast, it can even be punished by law. Glorbeasts are very loving and careful creatures to their children and companions. They lay many eggs each season that produce a large family of Glorbits that they will be extremely protective of. When a Glorbeast is angry, it can be a very fast and dangerous creature. Its electric shocks can reach targets up to 100 meters away and kill upon impact. Some floss in history have tried to utilize the Glorbeast's attack as a weapon, but the Glorbeasts are stubborn and too intelligent to be manipulated.

Stella Pi

2025 . 2nd Wave . Pre-Evolved . Electricity

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Stella Pis!
The Stella Pis Celebrate their Pet Day on June 14th
You can claim a free Stella Pi on that day in our discord server!

The Stella Pi are Thymono native little critters. They are soft and playful and love to climb and play. They are omnivores who eat small bugs and plants and are known to fish for minnows and little frogs! These critters move in packs of 10-20 and burrow into the ground in tunnel systems amid the moon's many craters. They will create a small volcano like hill structure made of dirt and rocks around the entrances to their tunnels. They are born from football-sized eggs that give off a bright glow!

The Stella Pi make small chittering sounds that can be described as sounding similar to electric popping. They are known to carry massive amounts of static electricity in their fur that will zap anything that comes too close to them! If you want to keep one as a pet, its important to treat their fur with special baths to keep them from harboring too much electricity at once!

The Candyfloss just fell in love with the Stella Pi's big eyes, adorable fur, and loving, cuddly personalities! These critters are a delight to have around now that they have come to the planet!
