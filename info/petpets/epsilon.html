Paizo Beast

2020 . 1st Wave . Evolved . Telepathy

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Paizo Beasts!
The Paizo Beasts Celebrate their Pet Day on July 29th
You can claim a free Paizo Beast on that day in our discord server!

In the wild they run in packs and their strength in numbers and hard plating on their bodies are essential for defense against deadly predators. Their strength and loyalty are one of their biggest attributes and their eye-catching intricate coat patterns make it easy to understand why they have long been sought as domesticated companions. Removing a Paizo Beast from the wild may not be the easiest task but luckily Paizo Pups are available and quickly trained to be a Candyfloss' best friend. When a Paizo Pup experiences true happiness it will evolve into your very own Paizo Beast!

Their large size may be domineering at first but these loveable doggos will snuggle themselves right into your heart. If you don't mind fur in your food or sleeping on less than a third of your bed than a Paizo Beast is perfect for you! With the right training, Candyfloss can even ride atop their Paizo Beast and experience what it feels like to truly run with the wind blowing through your hair across rolling fields of flowers. It has been rumored that when a particularly strong bond is made between a Paizo Beast its master there is an unbreakable bond formed that can include telepathic communication but the only way to know for sure would be loved one yourself! Paizo Beasts are excellent with kittens and easily house broken so why not take one home today!

Kinnantas

2021 . 2nd Wave . Pre-Evolved . Telepathy

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Kinnantass!
The Kinnantass Celebrate their Pet Day on January 23rd
You can claim a free Kinnantas on that day in our discord server!

These adorable furry elephants are a staple of Epsilon culture. The Kinnantas are common in south and East Epsilon and are found wandering the hilly plains in the wilds between villages. The Kinnantas have large flat feet and long trunks, and around their heads they have one long single ear that flows like a veil around their heads. Young Kinnantas are known to clumsily trip on their veil ear when they playfully run about. At the end of their trunks, they have Epsilon shaped skin folds that pull back to open their mouths.

The Kinnantas' loud trumpeting can be heard across great planes, as they call to their herd. Though they look shut, their eyes are actually open, but they have long thick lashes that protect their eyes from the sun. Kinnantas are known to have intricate henna-like patterns in their fur. The multiple eyes sometimes found on a Kinnantas are said to be able to see into a person's mind and tell if they are a good person or if they have devilish thoughts.

Herds of Kinnantas show signs of cognitive communication with one another, acting almost as a hive mind in the wild. Even in the home a Kinnantas may act as if they know what their owners think and feel and will sometimes imitate them. It is said that the henna designs and veil-like ear of the Kinnantas is what influenced similar elements in Epsilon fashion. Kinnantas can grow from the size of a puppy to up to three feet tall, so if you are keeping one as a pet be sure to have lots of space for them to live comfortably.

Kinmaerifas

2022 . 2nd Wave . Evolved . Telepathy

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Kinmaerifass!
The Kinmaerifass Celebrate their Pet Day on September 22nd
You can claim a free Kinmaerifas on that day in our discord server!

The booming sound of the Kinmaerifas can be heard echoing through the cliffs of Epsilon. This herding creature is said to have carved the very land of Epsilon and their roaring trumpets ring out in the hillside as they travel through the region. They are always on the move, never staying in one valley for too long.

The Kinmaerifas are large beasts, but always walk on their 4 hind legs, keeping their front legs pressed together, in a gesture that has become sacred to the Epsilon. It's said that the Kinmaerifas are all knowing beasts, who understand things about the world and life that the floss could never comprehend. Legend has it the eye that rests on their chests' can see into the past and future and informs their actions. Many go to the Kinmaerifas for advice and guidance. They are a beloved creature by the Epsilon and are protected by the clan leader. They are less like pets, and more like companions, though even if you befriend one its likely going to come and go as it pleases.

The Kinmaerifas cannot speak but can communicate with floss through telepathy by showing them images and expressing their emotions to them. The Kinmaerifas are gentle giants and only harm floss or predators when they are attacked and there is no other way to deter them. Though, even when threatened the Kinmaerifas prefer to out smart their enemies when able. These huge furry elephants have a thin piece of skin that flows behind their heads, like one big veil.

The Kinmaerifas' veil ear always appears like it's being blown by wind, flowing and gliding behind them elegantly. At the end of their long trunk they have a flap that acts as their mouth, that can open and close. It resembles the Epsilon symbol. The small wings that grow out of their back cannot lift them much off the ground, but have been seen to be used to lift the Kinmaerifas from a sitting to standing position, or aide them when going down declines.

Poulance

2023 . 3rd Wave . Un-Evolved . Telepathy

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Poulances!
The Poulances Celebrate their Pet Day on November 26th
You can claim a free Poulance on that day in our discord server!

The Poulance stand knee high on a Floss, and though they may appear birdlike, they are actually mammals and are incapable of flight. They do sometimes flap their arm feathers to balance themselves or to kick up water or dust to help clean their plumage. The Poulance are usually docile but if one feels threatened it will hunch down, fan its impressive tail and attack! So be careful!

These creatures run in flocks, and when it's mating time, they will compete with one another by dancing and showing off their brightly colored feathers and tails. Because of this dance, the Epsilon call them 'Ballarina Birds' affectionately. The Poulance is an omnivore and love to chase around small Jawbreakers and Rainblos. They use a telepathic link with their flock to warn each other of danger or alert the others when food is near by. Though they would be crushed by a bigger floss, they don't mind giving rides to Kittenfloss or honeys.

Many honeys in Chi like to ride atop the Poulances. It's common to see Poulance rides advertised as an attraction for the little ones in holidays and events.

Phoelance

2024 . 3rd Wave . Evolved . Telepathy

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Phoelances!
The Phoelances Celebrate their Pet Day on August 8th
You can claim a free Phoelance on that day in our discord server!

Unlike their pre-evolved form, the Phoelance can soar effortlessly through the sky. These massive birds are rare to see, and Epsilon legend says if you are able to spot one in the wild, that you will be blessed and given good luck. Finding a Phoelance feather can ensure the luck lasts while it's in your possession. The Phoelance are highly intelligent creatures, and some legends even say they communicate by implanting memories into the minds of floss who encounter them.

They are regal and fierce birds, known to lift their pray up into the air with ease and fly them off to their nests on the tops of the Epsilonion cliff sides. The iconic 'kaw' of the Phoelance can sometimes be heard echoing through the mountains from far off.

Very few floss in all of history have been able to form a bond with a Phoelance, however one of the current princes of Epsilon has a particular affinity with the bird and can even be seen riding atop it through the capital. When he takes his beloved partner to town, many people beg to pet the bird, knowing it may be their only chance to ever touch one.

Leoryx

2025 . 4th Wave . Un-Evolved . Telepathy

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Leoryxes!
The Leoryxes Celebrate their Pet Day on March 7th
You can claim a free Leoryx on that day in our discord server!

The Leoryx are a common animal to see on the cliffs of Epsilon. They are larger than most other feline pets and spend most of their lives climbing the sheer cliff sides and living in caves. They have a remarkable ability t use their six hooves to cling to the smallest of footholds, allowing them to climb up nearly completely vertical surfaces.

Their thick fur and bushy mane down their backs helps keep them warm in the snowy landscapes of Northern Pzekazi. They hunt Peolances, rodents, and are known to catch small Piaofu Ci with their claws out of the winding rivers and streams in their mountainous home.

Unlike the mighty Charion and Gatakeiros, the Leryx don't roar or meow, instead their make a trilling sound that reverberates thru the hills and cliffs to help them identify one another.

Domesticated Leoryx are known to dislike living indoors exclusively. They love to climb on rooftops and sleep in high places. They can be cuddly pets if you keep them well fed and loved but they can do some real damage if you mistreat them r let them get too pent up indoors. It's recommended to take them on regular hikes if you own one, and to install shelves for them to climb onto your walls.

Geolynx

2026 . 4th Wave . Evolved . Telepathy

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Geolynxes!
The Geolynxes Celebrate their Pet Day on November 3rd
You can claim a free Geolynx on that day in our discord server!

High atop the sheer cliffs of the Epsilonian mountains, the Geolynx reigns as a hardy sentinel. Evolved from the smaller Leorynx, these goat-like climbers are easily identified by their massive horns and the booming, haunting screams they use to communicate across vast valleys.

Adapted to the harshest peaks, they possess an impressive digestive system capable of processing not just mountain grasses, but even the raw metals and rocks. Physically, the Goelynx is a marvel of mountain evolution. While they often walk upright on their hind legs to scan the horizon, they drop onto all six limbs to achieve incredible speed or to scale vertical rock faces with ease. Their powerful hooves and sharp horns serve as dual-purpose tools, used both to excavate root vegetables from the frozen mountaintops and to defend against their predators like the Charion or the Wykorr.

The Geolynx is defined by a deeply protective nature. They move in disciplined herds, often acting as surrogate guardians for groups of ten or twenty Leorynx, even adopting those orphaned by predators. This natural instinct makes them invaluable to Candyfloss farmers, who regularly station a few Goelynx within their own flocks to ensure their livestock remains safe while grazing in the open mountain passes.
