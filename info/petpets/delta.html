Pyshokythi

2019 . 1st Wave . Pre-Evolved . Plant Manipulation

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Pyshokythi!
The Pyshokythis Celebrate their Pet Day on July 12th
You can claim a free Pyshokythi on that day in our discord server!

Revered by the Delta, the Psyhokythi are very special astral creatures. The Psyhokythi bring life to the planet and grow flowers in their wake. It's believed that the Psyhokythi can move freely between the mortal realm and the afterlife, and that they guide the souls of the deceased to the next life.

On the night of Vólta Stous Táfous the Floss pay tribute to these majestic creatures by leaving plastic pumpkins filled with Candies on the graves of their lost family members.

The Psyhokythi are very skittish and hate bright lights. Like a hermit crab, the Psyhokythi carry their homes around with them in the form of uniquely shaped gourds. No one quite knows why they take a liking to Pumpkins, but a Psyhokythi has never been seen without one. As far as the Floss are aware there's no way to separate a Psyhokythi from its gourd. Though they seem to be ethereal the Psyhokythi do have solid bodies beneath the mist they give and often give of wispy cooing sounds.

Psyhoecho

2010 . 1st Wave . Evolved . Plant Manipulation

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Psyhoechos!
The Psyhoechos Celebrate their Pet Day on May 18th
You can claim a free Psyhoecho on that day in our discord server!

Harbingers of change and spirits of the wild, calling a PsyhoEcho a petpet is pretty misleading. Its extremely rare for PsyhoEcho to appear to a Candyfloss let alone stick around long enough to be tamed. There have only been a select few ever recorded in history and some floss believe they are a myth. The Delta knows, however, how real and powerful the PsyhoEcho truly are.

It is believed that a Psyhokythi evolves into a PsyhoEcho, but it hasn't been proven, as no floss has ever witnessed this happening, though it's been said that PsyhoEchos have been spotted toting around the decorated Pumpkin that once belonged to a local Psyhokythi.

The ancient Delta texts describe the PsyhoEcho as great spirits of life and energy. It says that they are responsible for the changing of the seasons, the waning of the moon and even giving life itself to the creatures that inhabit Caramella. If a Floss is able to find and encounter a PsyhoEcho the experience will stick with them for their entire life.

Ostomagi

2021 . 2nd Wave . Pre-Evolved . Plant Manipulation

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Ostomagis!
The Ostomagis Celebrate their Pet Day on August 12th
You can claim a free Ostomagi on that day in our discord server!

These creatures are thought to be made of the bones of a kappa bear or other similar creatures. These bones are taken over by spirits who want a body. It is thought to be the same entity as the Psyhokythi and Psyhoecho. They are sacred, well respected, and thought to be able to curse you. The Delta believe that they gave them the earth that they live on and farm and therefore must be revered even though they cause mischief and have been known to abduct children.

Ostomagi are wandering spirits and love to travel around even if they don't have a destination in mind. They do actually like to be pets even if the Delta don't like to think of them like that. It is always obvious where one has been since they leave dead patches on the ground wherever they walk like they are sucking the energy from the very earth, which they are. They collect their energy from the plant life around them, even bothering their Deltafloss owners to grow them plants to “eat”. They are always glowing and using spiritual energy like it never runs out. There are people who want to harness this energy, but Delta does not allow it due to the Ostomagi's holiness to them.

Ostoreaper

2022 . 2nd Wave . Evolved . Plant Manipulation

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Ostoreapers!
The Ostoreapers Celebrate their Pet Day on February 3rd
You can claim a free Ostoreaper on that day in our discord server!

A massive skeleton makes up the base of the Ostoreaper. When it's sleeping or trying to hide it can collapse itself into a pile of bones and mute it's presence, blending itself in with its surroundings. Adventurers who pass through the Delta's The Plains of Charmont where they are known to gather, are often surprised by the Ostoreapers who lay in wait for their prey. Some Deltas consider these beasts to be the embodiment of spirits that are wandering Caramella and revere and fear them. They are rare creatures and are not likely to be seen in a lifetime, unless you travel to Delta's capital where one particular Hero is known to ride his Ostoreaper through the roads of Storlocke.

The Ostoreapers get their name from an old Delta legend that claims these creatures come for the souls of the dying to take them into the afterlife. According to that tale they say that if you make eye contact with a Reaper for too long then it means they have marked you for death! Some who have come across and Ostoreaper and lived, claim that the sounds they make sound like the whispers of ghosts and the light in their chest cavities seems to swirl and move and can even appear like faces at times!

Some think the light is composed of the souls the Osto has taken and uses for energy! Lots of tales tell of the Ostoreaper but the only way to know the truth is to tame one yourself... are you brave enough?

Wykorr

2023 . 3rd Wave . Un-Evolved . Plant Manipulation

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Wykorrs!
The Wykorrs Celebrate their Pet Day on April 15th
You can claim a free Wykorr on that day in our discord server!

The Wykorr are gnarly beasts who rule the skies over the vast wilds of Delta. Wykorr stand chest high on a Trufflefloss and can easily take down an unsuspecting Candyfloss if they set their sights on them. Often hunted when they come too close to civilizations, Wykorr are not common pets in the slightest.

These territorial, aggressive beasts can only be domesticated by the most fearless and strong floss who dare to capture and tame one. Wykorr are powerful creatures who can be useful as companions to Deltan adventurers. They are very high maintenance however and can only thrive in the Deltan region's temperate environment and unique fauna. With this fact, it's extremely rare to see one outside of their Clan territory. The Wykorr are covered in metallic plate like scaled on their stomachs that protect them from predators, and their winged arms can carry them from the ground to high in the clouds in seconds. They are dangerous foes and move in packs of up to 10 or 12 at a time. They communicate with their pack with a very distinct clicking noise that they make with their teeth called stridulating.

Wyrmkorr

2024 . 3rd Wave . Evolved . Plant Manipulation

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Wyrmkorrs!
The Wyrmkorrs Celebrate their Pet Day on June 27th
You can claim a free Wyrmkorr on that day in our discord server!

The Wyrmkorr are vicious, dangerous creatures. They are reptilian beasts that fly and run at high speeds on their four hoofed feet. Their upper arms are attached to web-like wings that allow them to gain height and fly for short periods of time. These heavy dragons are masters of defense, with hard metallic plating that grows naturally over their necks and bellies. The cross-shaped plate on their chest is a metallic plate that grows over top of their soul gem, further protecting them from enemies!

These are the only petpet that have a fully covered gem! Destroying their gem is still the most recommenced way to kill a Wyrmkorr but it takes a lot of skill and raw power to break off or through their protective covering! Knights of Delta have trained for many decades to fight Wyrmkorr to protect the citizens who live in Delta. The Wyrmkorr are floss-eaters and will attack and eat pretty much anything that moves! They are fast, sharp, and tough to defeat, but on top of all of that they are also poisonous! The claws that grow from the Wyrmkorr's wing-arms and their teeth secrete a toxic acid that will burn through metal and stone and if it gets on the skin it will poison the floss or other creature and slowly kill them! Thankfully the herbalists of Delta have a cure for the poison, but if you don't have it on hand, facing off with a Wyrmkorr is extremely deadly and best left to the adventurers and monster hunters!

Wyrmkorr are obsessed with shiny metals and will go out of their way to gather and hoard treasure, weapons and gold in their dens! They will spit acid onto the metal to melt it and shape it into nests, and they're known to coat their eggs in this melted metal to protect them from predators and the elements! The Wyrmkorr do not roar like most dragon-type creatures, but instead they make a very distinct clicking sound with their throats! If you ever hear this sinister clicking and whirring be sure to hide! Sometimes offering a sacrifice for the Wyrmkorr to eat will give you enough time to hide! Running and fighting may be futile but the Wyrmkorr are not especially good at tracking or hunting so the best thing to do is to get out of their line of sight and try to stay very quiet!

Crow Scurry

2025 . 4th Wave . Un-Evolved . Plant Manipulation

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Crow Scurrys!
The Crow Scurrys Celebrate their Pet Day on March 23rd
You can claim a free Crow Scurry on that day in our discord server!

The autumnal forests and wide plains of Delta are home to one of the region’s most beloved but fragile residents: the Crow Scurry. These small, furry creatures are inseparable from their environment, not just because they live off the land, but because their very survival depends on a unique, personal form of protection. The Crow Scurry is incredibly sensitive to the cold; even moderate room temperatures can prove fatal.

To combat this vulnerability, they have become master engineers of mobile warmth. Scurries are famous for weaving ingenious sack-like structures from materials gathered in their environment—showing a strong preference for dried plants and hay. Each sack is a meticulously crafted cocoon, which they stuff with leaves, soft fluff, and dried wheat to maximize insulation. Every design includes perfectly sized holes where the Scurry can press its face out, taking in the world while staying perfectly cozy inside its mobile home.

Given their love for hay and wheat, Crow Scurries thrive around the farms and fields of Delta, and the farmers couldn't be happier about it. The Scurries only eat dried, dead weeds and old vegetables, meaning they pose absolutely no threat to valuable crops. In fact, they are the loyal protectors of the harvest. Energetic and quick, they dedicate themselves to chasing away birds and other small critters that might threaten their territory. The local farmers, recognizing their dedication, often build high platforms specifically for Scurries to perch upon, offering them the best vantage point for guarding the fields. It's a charming local custom to place a tiny scarf or an old hat around the perched scurry and its sack, effectively turning the diligent little creature into a living, fuzzy scarecrow.

This dependence on their warm, woven home means that maintenance is critical. If a Scurry's sack rips or tears, the immediate loss of insulation can be deadly. Delta farmers frequently check on their tiny neighbors, taking the time to carefully sew up and patch any weak spots, ensuring the creature remains healthy and warm. Though they are generally energetic and docile when well-cared for, Scurries require immense devotion if kept as household pets, thriving only in the warmest of indoor environments. Strict rules apply to their care: the Scurry must remain in its mobile home at all times. It should only be taken out temporarily for necessary functions, such as cleaning the sack's fillings, swapping the creature to a new home, or for a quick bath, before being immediately placed back into its comfortable mobile cocoon.

Whether perched high above a wheat field, zealously guarding the season's yield, or tucked safely into a freshly repaired sack, the Crow Scurry remains the energetic, crucial, and entirely charming centerpiece of agricultural life in Delta.
