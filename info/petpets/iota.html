Mikro Oplo

2019 . 1st Wave . Pre-Evolved . Nullification

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Mikro Oplo!
The Mikro Oplos Celebrate their Pet Day on May 21st
You can claim a free Mikro Oplo on that day in our discord server!

Renowned for being used by the Iota Mafia in cage fights, the Mikró óplo are actually very gentle creatures unless specifically trained to fight. The Mikró óplo can be found all over the city from pet shops to dumpsters and are in need of a loving home!

The Mikró óplo have the special ability to negate any power within range of them, which makes them a force to be reckoned with. These feathery creatures usually waddle on their hind legs but when running or jumping they crawl on all fours. Despite having wings the Mikró óplo cannot fly but can use them to glide down from high buildings. Usually, they drag their wings behind them when they waddle.

Makro Oplo

2020 . 1st Wave . Evolved . Nullification

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Makro Oplos!
The Makro Oplos Celebrate their Pet Day on November 18th
You can claim a free Makro Oplo on that day in our discord server!

Big, Beautiful. Bossy. These lovable bundles of feathers and sass are the evolution of the Iota's Mikro Oplo. Not so Micro anymore the Makro Oplos are waist high on a trufflefloss and can weigh up to 300 lbs.! woah! With diamond deposits growing from their fur these pets are top dollar companions that are usually owned by the wealthiest of floss. Makro Oplo are needy creatures, having to be kept in, brushed daily and given special diets. While cuddly and tolerant to their owners, these birbs can be right brats, if they dent get what they want they won't hesitate to knock over furniture and squawk loudly all while giving you the eyes of death. But damn if they're not soft and adorable.

Parent Makros will sometimes hide their Mikro infants under their large cape like wings for warmth and protection. If you can't find your Kittenfloss check under the Makro. While they usually prefer to walk on their hind legs like adults, the Makro are known to run on all fours like their younger evolutions if they are in danger, and if they're lucky they might be able to get a few feet of height from their wings - if they're in shape that is.

Knan Nibble

2021 . 2nd Wave . Pre-Evolved . Nullification

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Knan Nibbles!
The Knan Nibbles Celebrate their Pet Day on June 10th
You can claim a free Knan Nibble on that day in our discord server!

The Knan'Nibblers are small to medium sized city dwelling creatures. They can be found in dumpsters, alleyways, and trash cans, wherever their favorite food, garbage, can be found. Generally thought of as gross, due to their diet, Knan'Nibblers aren't kept as pets except in Iota Clan even though they are found in most of the large cities on Carmella. These creatures like to gnash their teeth and will eat anything put in front of their mouth, even non-edible things! They are naturally vicious, living in large nests that will attack as a group if they think they can win. It's a hotly debated topic, but it's believed that if there were enough Knan'Nibblers around, they could probably take down a Flogasaurus Rex. They can be trained to not be violent but will still be very protective and bare their teeth and hiss at strangers and won't get along with other Petpets and kittens.

They have a special trait where when viewed in low light, they can look like shadows, a shifting shape and some eyes. It's very hypnotic and if a Floss or other petpet locks eyes with a Knan'Nibbler, the creature can turn off their abilities. They use this power a lot when hunting though it doesn't help against Mikro Oplos which hunt them. They can be seen battling quite often and it usually doesn't end well for the Nibblers. If the Knan'Nibblers are tame, they like to keep themselves clean. However, they don't care about cleanliness in the wild. It's a common saying that even if one is a tame petpet they might eat their owner if they have passed, it's never been proved though.

Knan Snap

2022 . 2nd Wave . Evolved . Nullification

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Knan Snaps!
The Knan Snaps Celebrate their Pet Day on Februaru 11th
You can claim a free Knan Snap on that day in our discord server!

The Knan'Snaps are huge, strong rodents with long slinking bodies and strong snapping jaws. They are rare to see in the wild but are found sparingly haunting the dark back alleys of Iota. They love garbage and will scarf down just about anything without much thought. They are viscous creatures who tend to be dangerous when encountered. They are often hunted and exterminated when found but they breed rapidly making it hard to keep their numbers low. Knan'Snaps will chatter their sharp teeth to communicate with one another and will be quick to bite and rip apart anything in their way, so be careful! They live in colonies and nests that are usually comprised of several hundred Knan'Nibbles and only one or two Knan'Snaps.

Unsurprisingly they don't make good pets and tend to stay wild. Much like the Nibbles, Snaps' eyes are somewhat hypnotic and can seem like they shift in color if you look into them too deeply. If they lock eyes with prey or floss they can quench their powers and make it even harder to catch them. The Snaps emit a smoke-like darkness from their bodies to shroud themselves and their nests in darkness. Unlike the Nibbles and Mikro Oplo, the Knan'Snaps don't pay much mind to Makro Oplos and tend to stay away from each other.

"Tame" Knan'Snaps can be viewed at zoos and circuses but these dangerous creatures are also used by the Iota Mafia to terrorize their enemies.

Ioneshark

2024 . 3rd Wave . Un-Evolved . Nullification

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Ionesharks!
The Ionesharks Celebrate their Pet Day on December 17th
You can claim a free Ioneshark on that day in our discord server!

The many waterways and sewer systems of Iota are home to the viscous and terrifying Ioneshark! These semi-aquatic chompers are slick smooth sharks that swim at speed up to 20 mph and gobble up anything that moves! They have strong jaws that can cut off a floss' leg in a single chomp and are known to attack floss who fall into the river surrounding Iota's border. Many floss who leap from the bridges (or are pushed) are said to be going to "feed the Ionesharks", and it's a common threat for gangsters to use when trying to scare people. The Ionesharks can chase their prey out of the water and are known to come ashore and run around on their hind legs causing mayhem. Thankfully they're much slower and not well coordinated when running on land and will die if they are away from water for too long.

Unlike normal teeth that can fall out, rot, or be pulled, the Ionesharks have evolved to have internal teeth covered in strong razor shark skin! That way they can chomp right through trash, metal, and bone without any concern for what they're putting in their mouths.

They will eat just about anything! Including you! It's not unheard of for owners of Ionesharks to get a bit too comfortable with their dangerous pet and end up on the dinner menu. Those who choose to keep them usually do so in a large tank that they keep their distance from.

The Anibolds are thigh high bipedal pets Like many pets they are quite smart and able to do simple tasks and understand speech to some degree. They are often used as guards in Alpha and are often found in dungeons and temples in East Alpha. The Anibolds make great protectors for families, and other pets. They are brave, bold and fierce. While they prefer to walk on their back legs it's not uncommon to see one run on all 6 when trying to move quickly.

They move in packs in the wild with a leader Anibold that governs its pack and the Anijuru under them. The leader Anibold is often challenged by the others in fights to determine new leadership. When trained by a family, they are amazing pets. Their soft fur and obedient nature make them great for any home. They are gentle with children and tend to treat other pets well if they are given a place of authority over them. They are known to discipline other pets when they act out of line. In the wild, Anibolds often fight one another for dominance.

Even if they are well trained, they will fight another Anibold or aggressive petpets when they come across them. To help them get their wiggles out while being safe about it - some places in Alpha have Anibold tournaments. This is a safe place where the Anibolds and other pets can fight one another without killing or mortally wounding each other. A lot of them train to be the best and actively strive to win these tournaments. It's rare but sometimes wild Anibolds are known to wander into the tournaments to fight without a Candyfloss representing them.
