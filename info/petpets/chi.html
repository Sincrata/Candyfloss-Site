Spraoiling

2023 . 1st Wave . Pre-Evolved . Minification

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Spraoiling!
The Spraoilings Celebrate their Pet Day on April 7th
You can claim a free Spraoiling on that day in our discord server!

The Spraoiling (Spree-ling) are remarkable creatures. They're docile leaf eaters who roam the dome-land of Chi. They're slow-moving critters who have many legs and keep low to the ground. They wear protective "shells" on their backs that they can recoil into when they're in danger or frightened. Their shell will often take different forms to match their environment for better protection. The Spraoilings can use minification to change their size.

Their natural size is quite large in fact, coming in around 15 feet tall at their full height. When at this large of a size the Spraoiling are often used as mounts for the Chifloss, and can often be found pulling carriages or carts of supplies across the hills of Chi. Though they CAN be large, the Spraoiling often WANT to be small, extremely small in fact, they are most often found between 2 feet tall all the way to several inches in height. Their bodies come in slick and furred variations that help them to adapt to conditions in any clan. The little Spraoiling are affectionate, easy to care for and make for fabulous pets all across Caramella.

Skoinkling

2024 . 1st Wave . Evolved . Minification

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Skoinklings!
The Skoinklings Celebrate their Pet Day on January 15th
You can claim a free Skoinkling on that day in our discord server!

Unlike their slow moving and docile previous forms, the Skoinklings are fast moving critters who scurry around on their eight legs, hauling their shells on their lanky backs. They are able to scurry up vertical surfaces like plants and walls and can even hang upside down under the hat of a mushroom or the gutters of houses. Their sensitive eyes have no lids, so the Skoiklings are known to lick their eyes with their long elastic tongues to keep them moist.

Their tongues can shoot out of their mouths to catch bugs or leaves and zip them into their mouths. While the Spraoiling are naturally large, and use their minification abilities to become small, the Skoinkling are actually the opposite. Their natural state of being is quite small, able to be held in the arms of a floss, but when they use their growth abilities, they can become very large - well over the size of a floss and are used to ride and pull carriages. Some carriages are even fit to be carried up vertical surfaces by the Skoinklings. These large lizard-like beings are friendly and easy to tame. They are playful and loving and easily grow bonds with floss who take care of them.

Bumblegrinn

2025 . 2nd Wave . Pre-Evolved . Minification

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Bumblegrinns!
The Bumblegrinns Celebrate their Pet Day on February 23rd

From the enchanting land of Chi hails the Bumblegrinn, a creature as delightful as its name suggests. These furry, friendly critters are a common and cherished part of life for the Chi, known for their unique blend of playful charm and incredible adaptability. Instantly recognizable by their distinct features, Bumblegrinn are flying beings, propelled through the air by thin insect wings, sporting characteristically big butts and long, expressive antennae.

Being skittish, they don't like confronting larger creatures, including Floss, making them hard to tame since they curl up into a ball when spooked and are very fast to getaway. They are sensitive to the cold and are recommended to be kept only in Alpha or under heat lamps. The Ammos Skippers are omnivores and like to eat bugs and desert grass when they can find it. They are choice meals for the Flogasaurus Rex and do not get along with Floga Drakai because if they have enough stamina to complete the chase, they will also eat the Ammos Skippers.

These antennae are far more than mere decoration; they serve as sophisticated sensory organs, allowing the Bumblegrinn to perceive their environment with acute detail and to emote a wide range of feelings, making them wonderfully communicative companions.

Using their power of size manipulation, they're able to shrink and grow at will. This allows them to vary from a large size, about knee-high on an adult popfloss, to as utterly diminutive as an Earth bee. They relish changing their size frequently, a playful habit that adds to their endearing nature. Chi owners of these captivating pets sometimes shrink themselves and enjoy riding their larger Bumblegrinn high above the trees, offering breathtaking views of the Chi landscape.

Despite their robust appearance and ability to carry riders, Bumblegrinn are surprisingly fragile creatures. This inherent vulnerability has led to a fascinating evolutionary adaptation: they instinctively look and act cute for the honeyfloss, a symbiotic relationship where the protective honeyfloss guard them from potential predators. Their diet consists primarily of the nectar and pollen of various flowers, making them natural pollinators, and a sweet treat of sugar is always a welcome delight.

Reproduction among Bumblegrinn is a spectacle of nature, as they birth massive litters, often numbering in the thousands. While this ensures a plentiful population, it also necessitates careful management. Breeding Bumblegrinn is a restricted practice, with most individuals required to be neutered or spayed to maintain population control. Only specific individuals, known as Queen or King Bumblegrinn, are permitted to lay and fertilize eggs, ensuring the continued, yet regulated, vitality of the species. Beyond their practical roles and unique biology, Bumblegrinn are deeply affectionate creatures, loving to cuddle and engage in playful antics, making them truly beloved members of the Chi ecosystem.
