Jawbreaker Bat

Common Petpet . No Evolution . No Power

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Jawbreaker Bat!
The Jawbreaker Bats Celebrate their Pet Day on July 23rd
You can claim a free Jawbreaker Bat on that day in our discord server!

Jawbreakers are cute little furballs who love to run and fly. With their four stout legs, they leap and scurry on the ground and with such puny wings, it's a wonder they can fly at all! They sleep most of the day and like to run in circles all night. Better keep an eye on them because despite their lack of eyesight hey are highly curious and love to chew on things!

Each Jawbreaker has a fluffy ball on its antennae that represents the clan they were bred in. Their colorful tongues can come in a variety of wacky patterns to match your Floss. They also come in different sizes from as small as a softball to as big as a bowling ball. They're very affectionate and make great lap-bets.

Rainblo

Common Petpet . No Evolution . No Power

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Rainblos!
The Rainblos Celebrate their Pet Day on March 17th
You can claim a free Rainblo on that day in our discord server!

Rainblos are found crawling and skittering around the entire world of Caramella. From the soft Rainblo in the north and Psekazi, to the scaly Glykozian Rainblo, to the smooth silky Rainblo found on the island Karpos. Rainblo are very docile and curious animals, growing to about the size of a football, these little critters make great petpets and love to be pampered. The Rainblo like to be in high places and tend to ride atop their Candyfloss owners and sleep on top of shelves.

Though it appears that Rainblo's are mouthless, they actually have small tunnel-like mouths that lead directly into their cavernous bellies. Rainblos live to keep their bellies full of their favorite food. When a Rainblo is young it chooses a food that is it's favorite then will refuse to eat anything else for the rest of its life. Rainblos seem to be able to consume just about anything, from candy, to metal, to lava and magma! woah! Over time the Rainblo will digest the items in its belly and will want more to fill it back up. No one is quite sure where the items go, as they seem to just disappear.

When a Rainblo is hungry it will let its owners know with a loud barrage of "MEEP!" and "MEP!"s, sometimes resulting to nipping at their owners in protest. It's always wise for Rainblo Owners to carry some food around with them.

Rainblo's dazzling eyes are the most like a floss' of all the petpets and some believe they are spiritually connected to the floss because of this. A Rainblo's pupils and the marking on their foreheads resemble the breed they are, that is usually indignant of the Clan they were bred in. Unlike Festival PetPets rainblos have no supernatural powers... unless you count eating just about anything.

Marsmallow

Common Petpet . No Evolution . No Power

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Marsmallows!
The Marsmallows Celebrate their Pet Day on September 4th
You can claim a free Marsmallow on that day in our discord server!

Marsmallows are adorable little fellows who are quiet and gentle. Marsmallows seem to have appeared one day on Caramella. Some floss believe they are visitors from outer space - but thats just nonsense. Marsmellowa are knee high on a sugarfloss and waddle on two legs like a sugarfloss. With long sensitive anttanae and little 3 fingered paws the Marsmellows very quickly stole the Candyfloss' hearts. These creatures are very dependant and dont last long without Candyfloss Intervention.

They dont seem to eat or have mouths at all, but somehow will perish without attention from a floss - perhaps from lack of affection? Marsmellows are found all over Caramella and come in a wide variety of coats and patterns. Their traits seem to reflect the floss from the areas they come from, with tech and fur traits in the north to fishy and gooey traits down south and a large clan shaped gem is embedded in their chests. It's unknown if this gem is aesthetic or soul bound like a floss' - no one has the heart to find out.

Marsmallows are highly curious and emotive creatures, who love to learn and seek the approval of their floss owners. Some equate owning a Marsmellow to raising a perpetual Kittenfloss.

Small slippery creatures with a curious nature and desire to be involved in all the goings on around them! Salakeios are great for those looking for a pet small enough to fit in the palm of your hand but who have big personalities to contrast their size! They love to explore and get into things so you better lock up anything you don't want them to get their sticky little hands on!

These amphibious little animals love the outdoors but can also maintain a happy indoor lifestyle provided you offer them enough mental stimulation and comforts of nature such as access to pools of water to swim in and things to climb! Salakeios are likely to be by your side whenever they can be just so they can see what you're up to or if they can help, you'll never feel lonely with one by your side
