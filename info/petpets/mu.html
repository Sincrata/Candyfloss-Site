Eros Moro

2021 . 1st Wave . Pre-Evolved . Healing

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Eros Moro!
The Eros Moros Celebrate their Pet Day on September 10th
You can claim a free Eros Moro on that day in our discord server!

These precious lambs are soft and gleeful and love to sprint through meadows of flowers and soar through the sunset clouds! Unlike the two headed Dyopaw the Eros Moro's heads are rarely at odds with each other. You will often find them licking each other's wounds and snuggling up to each other. They usually share their emotions both negative and positive. The Eros Moro live all over Glykos and often flock together in similar types.

Feathered Eros Moro are most common and are found dotted around grasslands and forests. Butterfly Eros tend to make their homes in flowering fields and mystical forests, Bat winged Eros tend to live in drier areas around Gamma and Alpha and are best suited to living in cities. Much like Mufloss the Eros Moro are known for their healing abilities. The snuggle of a Eros Moro can ease the most terrible of headaches and their nuzzles can even close wounds. They cannot be forced to use their abilities however so capturing them by force is not going to get you anywhere. They're illusive beasts but there have been instances where floss lost in the wilderness have survived thanks to the grace and love of the Eros Moros. Legends say that the Eros Moros are steeds to the Cherubfloss and live among them in the clouds - tho this isnt proven.

Eros Mythos

2022 . 1st Wave . Evolved . Healing

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Eros Mythoss!
The Eros Mythoss Celebrate their Pet Day on November 13th
You can claim a free Eros Mythos on that day in our discord server!

The Eros Mythos is, much like the name implies, almost a mythical creature. Only recently have the floss proved its existence and have been able to evolve Eros Moro into this form. It's difficult to raise and rare to find. The Eros Mythos is smaller than you might think, only about 5 feet high from the floor to the tops of their heads when standing. o 93 Kilometers per hour at their top speed, kicking up clouds of sand behind them.

A kittenfloss or particularly light Sugarfloss may be able to ride one but the rest of us are better off with a Petrinos! These regal creatures are calm and friendly if they know you but are otherwise loners and will run from unfamiliar floss. They are fast on the ground and in the sky! The Eros Moro love to gallop through the flower fields of mu and sour through the skies with their herds of Eros Moro.

It's not common to see multiple Eros Mythos together but they can be found among large groups of Moros. Their healing abilities are extremely potent, but the Mythos are very reluctant to use them, and no floss has been able to train them to do it on command. Even faced with a floss who is injured they will refuse to help unless it is certain that they will die. Some claim the Mythos can revive the recently deceased but this is not proven. As recent as a decade ago these creatures were talked about with an air of wonder and disbelief. It was said that this mythical beast would only appear to those who were near death lost in the forest of norther Mu. After blacking out the floss would find themselves in a bed of flowers near a settlement where they would have miraculously recovered. It's believed that their small size, remarkable healing abilities, and quick flight are responsible for tales like these.

Amouille Mourve

2023 . 2nd Wave . Pre-Evolved . Healing

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Amouille Mourves!
The Amouille Mourves Celebrate their Pet Day on October 17th
You can claim a free Amouille Mourve on that day in our discord server!

These palm sized pets make their homes in flooded wetlands all over Caramella but derive from the ponds and rivers of the Mu region. They are slimey and many find them gross or ugly, but if you can come to love them, their plump friend-shaped bodies give them an endearing charm and their quirky and harmless bahavoir make them great household pets. The Amouille Mourve are spoken of in old Mu legends, saying that there was once a Mu prince so obsessed with his looks that he was cursed by the dark spirits to live his life as a disgusting Mourve, unless someone could come to love him in this slimy ugly form. The legend says he was able to find love, and when kissed by a princess from Nu, he transformed back into his floss form, and they lived happily ever after.

Due to this legend, the Amouille Mourve has stood for love that overcomes outward appearances and adversity across all of Caramella. Some young Floss maidens still dream of kissing a Amoiulle Mourve and transforming him into a valiant prince. The Amouille Mourve have no back limbs and instead drag their sluggish bodies, which often leaves behind a gooey slime if they're not properly bathed.

The Amouille can use their strong hind quarters to launch themselves into the air. This form of dragging and jumping is their main way of transportation. Their sticky bodies can also adhere to vertical surfaces like walls and trees, and they can even hang from ceilings. They are omnivores and eat mostly algae and small plants but are known to sometimes eat grubs and flying insects. They are semiaquatic and like to bathe in shallow water from time to time. The lillypad shaped apendage that grows out of their head aids them in hiding just on the surface of the water. If you have one as a pet, it's wise to keep a tank for them to hang out in unless you are handling them.

Amoure Pog

2024 . 2nd Wave . Evolved . Healing

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Amoure Pogs!
The Amoure Pogs Celebrate their Pet Day on May 5th
You can claim a free Amoure Pog on that day in our discord server!



The Amoure Pog is the evolved form of the squishy and slimy Amouille Mourve from the Mu marshlands. These larger amphibians are rather rare to find in the wild. Legends say that Mourves will only evolve into Pogs if they receive a kiss from a beautiful maiden, though, this isn't proven. The Amoure Pog is a docile, gentle creature that lives in and out of the water. They are lazy fellas that will often hide just below the water level, allowing their lily pad-shaped heads to sit on the surface and blend them into their environments.

These critters are very flirtatious and love to carry flowers around with them to give to any 'sweet thang' that catches their eye. The Pogs have long, strong legs that they use to leap far distances. They can also waddle on two legs but prefer to use a hopping step to get around. On the belly of the Amoure Pog, they have a large sack of transparent skin that can expand and contract when they breathe. This sack is known to become filled up on occasion with various things the Pog has eaten like flowers, other plants, and even water. Some sacks have opaque coverings leading them to resemble a gigantic soul gem. There are large families of Amouille Mourves, but it's common to find a single Amoure Pog leading them. The Mourves are known to stick to the body of their daddy Pog and ride on their lily pads for protection and to show their allegiance and affection. Some Amouille Mourves are known to have kissy marks on their faces, and it's believed that these marks are the result of Pogs trying to help their little buddies to evolve.

Pettiplume

2025 . 3rd Wave . Un-Evolved . Healing

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Pettiplumes!
The Pettiplumes Celebrate their Pet Day on April 4th
You can claim a free Pettiplume on that day in our discord server!

Native to the Mu rivers and lakes, but found in many parts of the world, these water fowls come in many varieties unique to certain environments. Around the ocean its common to see "Pettipelicans" scooping up fish from the ocean in their deep beaks. In the jungles of Karpos, there are very brightly colored "Pettiparrots" who use their hook like beaks to pry open fruits and crack nuts. The long-beaked "Petticranes" are common in the bogs of middle and northern glykos.

Pink "Pettimingos" can be found in the oases of Alpha, and "Pettipidgeons" have adapted to living in parks in cities in Iota and Zeta.

Of all the Pettiplume breeds however, the "Pettiswans" of Mu are the very first, and the most beloved. In Mu, Pettiplumes are sacred by their premier religion, J'aime Tout, the worshipers of the Godess of Love. The Pettiplume are very affectionate creatures, and if you have one as a pet you can expect it to be very attention seeking and give lots of kisses and cuddles. They are pack critters and arent happy unless they have a mate or friend to prune and flock with. Pettiplumes are resilient birds that are known to be friendly towards those who feed and care for them, and vicious to anyone who crosses them or their young. They are commonly found in large flocks near bodies of water. They love to float on the surface of calm water, fly through the air in V-formations, and walk with their younglings in a neat little row.

Legends say the Pettiplumes were created by the Goddess of Lust, and are actually her little cupids in disguise. It's said that if a young couple is approached by a Pettiplume and they are kind to it, that Lust will bless their relationship. If a lonely-heart crosses paths with a Pettiplume it's said that the cupid will acknowledge them and send love their way soon. One legend even states that long-beaked Petticranes (sometimes called "Pettistorks") are sent down by the Goddess of Love in the heavens, to carry the souls of new Kittenfloss in a bundle and safely deliver them to expecting parents.

At night, flocks of Pettiplumes are known to make rhythmic calls in unison that are said to sound like the soft sobs of the Goddess Cherish, who weeps for those who are injured and lonely. The tears of a Pettiplume are well known to be potent healing elixirs. However they are hard to come by and extremely expensive because the birds are revered so highly and rarely shed tears in captivity when treated kindly. The most common time a Pettiplume will cry is when their children or mate is killed, and thanks to conservation efforts by the Mu government, that is pretty rare.
