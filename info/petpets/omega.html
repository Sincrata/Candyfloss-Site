Mimotari

2022 . 1st Wave . Pre-Evolved . Crystallokinesis

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Mimotari!
The Mimotaris Celebrate their Pet Day on July 17th
You can claim a free Mimotari on that day in our discord server!

These adorable bio-luminescent terrapins call the tunnels of Omega their home! Mimotari come in many forms, they are semi aquatic and like to stay near aquifers where glowing mushrooms abound. As a defense mechanism the Mimotari suck in their head, tail and limbs into their mushroom-like shell and nestle into the shrooms around their home, perfectly camouflaging themselves from predators.

The Mimotari differ in appearance depending on their environment but those deeper in the caves even have crystalline eyes for better dark vision bioluminescence is scarce. These lap-sized critters are skittish and low-energy and make great pets to the Omegafloss, and now, the rest of Caramella too! They would love nothing more than to sit on your laptop keys and snuggle under your blankets.

Though they look like mammals, Mimotari are actually fungus. They need only water to live but enjoy eating plants and bugs for extra nutrients. They mate by releasing spores and baby Mimotari are indistinguishable from regular mushrooms in early development before their limbs and head come out.

Gremotari

2023 . 1st Wave . Evolved . Crystallokinesis

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Gremotaris!
The Gremotaris Celebrate their Pet Day on September 8th
You can claim a free Gremotari on that day in our discord server!

Deep in the caves systems of Omega the Gremotari move in flocks, crawling through the tunnels, gathering up food and mosses and keeping the crystal caves clean! These slow-moving terrapins love to clean and eat grime and mosses that grow on the cave walls, and can even become anxious in a dirty environment, Floss in Omega like to keep them around to help things stay tidy! The Gremotari stand knee high but tend to hunch and walk on all fours when they need to move quickly.

They are timid creatures who are easily hunted and eaten by other cave dwelling monsters. Their large, fragile soul gem covers most of the chest, so they make a habit of hiding it to keep it safe. They are often found with smaller Mimotari clinging to their backs or heads, trying to blend in to thei surrounding mushrooms. Their large eyes are perfect for seeing in the dark cave systems and their nimble fingers and claws are used to dig up crystals and make burrows.

When they are frightened the Gremotari will retreat under their Mushroom-like shells to blend in with the bioluminescent mushrooms that litter the Omega caves. The mouths on the back of their shell is their only defense against predators so watch out if you're walking through the caves, if you step too close to a hiding Gremotari you may get bit! Though timid, and hard to train at first, once a floss earns a Gremotari's trust they are loyal and affectionate companions.

Nerafligh

2024 . 3rd Wave . Pre-Evolved . Crystallokinesis

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Neraflighs!
The Neraflighs Celebrate their Pet Day on May 26th
You can claim a free Nerafligh on that day in our discord server!

The Nerafligh were originally subterranean insect-like creatures but since the Earthquake that opened up Omega, the Nerafligh have been spreading all over Caramella. They stand about 4-5 inches tall. They have bird clawed feet and a loose sheet of flesh that separates from their under body at the neckline and flutters around them.

The underbody is soft and furry and the flesh around it is bald and leathery. Under their flesh they have mouth-like openings where they consume the plants and crystals that they eat.

The Nerafligh will land on its meal and then close its flesh around it while it consumes. Nerafligh have thin, insect-like wings that they beat rapidly to fly. They are very brittle and easy to harm or even kill. The Nerafligh make good pets to floss but are usually best kept in birdcages or they risk getting lost, stepped on, or wandering outside the home and being unable to find their way back. They are easy prey for all other pets, so they are very skittish and fragile.

Spirifligh

2025 . 3rd Wave . Evolved . Crystallokinesis

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Spiriflighs!
The Spiriflighs Celebrate their Pet Day on December 27th
You can claim a free Spirifligh on that day in our discord server!

With their long legs and neck, the Spirifligh are larger than most floss yet have no trouble moving around the tight tunnels of their Omegan home. They are fearsomely fast and nimble, and their cloth-like mouths can swallow up entire floss and trap them in their mouths. Their pointed beak-like noses are used to skewer their food or enemies and as such these creatures are feared by most floss and are known to be very dangerous.

That said, they can be trained and by those who are skilled in creature taming and taught to be gentler with floss as long as they're taken care of. They can be mounted by young or small floss and are useful in farming and travel in the tunnels of Omega. They are known to even be tamed by the wild Grimotari in the deeper tunnels of Omega. It's not unheard of to encounter a Grimotari mounted on the back of a Spirifligh! This makes these enemies extremely dangerous so be careful!

Though they appear like birds, they are actually gigantic insects who use their long noses like a stinger to poison their prey. The underside of their body is a mouth-like organ where they fold up their 'cloth' around a prey and hold them there while their bodies digest their food. If a floss is hunted by the Spirifligh they will be poisoned so that they become lethargic and sleepy then digested, so it's best to never go around alone in the habitat of the Spirifligh. Make sure you have a buddy to help pull you out of their belly before it's too late!
