Listen carefully for the purring of a Cháricat hiding within the flower bushes! These adorable kitties love to frolic and play in the Gardens of Beta. The Cháricat are mischievous little devils who will do anything for a spot of tea or the opportunity to knock a designer teacup off a table. The Cháricat have the natural ability to hover and float, and you will rarely find them sitting on the ground. They prefer to leap and bound through the air, leaving chaos in their wake. Befriend one of these devils and they will be your cuddliest companions. The Cháricat love nothing greater than a warm lap to curl up in. However, they do not like being dominated!

Be warned if you adopt a Cháricat they will still love to wreck things from time to time and demand your attention when they want it! It's hard to stay mad at them though -- I mean, just look at those eyes!

Charion

2021 . 1st Wave . Evolved . Levitation

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Charions!
The Charions Celebrate their Pet Day on July 14th
You can claim a free Charion on that day in our discord server!

Meet the Charion, kings of the Beta region! These big cats are ferocious hunters that rule over the rest of the animal kingdom. Charions tend to have an air of grace and arrogance about them and will often hover and refuse to walk on a floor that is dirty or unfamiliar to them.

These glorious beasts can be tamed only if they are raised around floss from birth and are otherwise fierce and dangerous. However captive Charions are big lazy fur babies and love to cuddle and play. They are the boss of their owner's household and when they want your attention you will be bound to give it to them, despite the best efforts by Candyfloss Charion refuse to be trained and tend to act out against floss they are annoyed with. They are great with Kittenfloss however and love to snuggle in the night. Mother Charion will carry their Charicat kittens by the scruff of their neck and like to bathe in sunlight.

Piao-Fu Ci

2022 . 2nd Wave . Pre-Evolved . Levitation

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Piao-Fu Cis!
The Piao-Fu Cis Celebrate their Pet Day on August 2nd
You can claim a free Piao-Fu Ci on that day in our discord server!

The PiaoFu-Ci are a beloved creature native only to the Beta region and parts of Theta. The FiaoFu-Ci never stop growing. They hatch from eggs about the size of a baseball and grow at a steady rate. The average Piaofu-Ci lives to be about the size of a Paizo dog, but the largest in history was said to be bigger than a Flogasaurus! PiaoFu-Ci come in many varieties depending on their breed, but the most common are the Porcelain PiaoFu-Ci whose body is made of slick milky glass and can be painted and decorated. The PiaoFu-Ci are docile creatures that do not attack Floss or other pets, but sometimes will fight one another for territory. Its recommended to not have two together who arent mates.

The Piafu-Ci are remarkable pets who use their levitation abilities to float in the air at about eye level. They dont usually go much higher, and can land on the ground only to sleep. They do enjoy swimming however its not necessary for them to be in or around water, as they breathe air and eat bugs.

Longfu-Yu

2023 . 2nd Wave . Evolved . Levitation

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Longfu-Yus!
The Longfu-Yus Celebrate their Pet Day on September 13th
You can claim a free Longfu-Yu on that day in our discord server!

The Longfu-Yu are a very rare creature, only several are known to exist and they are spoken of often in Beta legends. The Longfu-Yu are said to be ever growing longer. Their tails can stretch on for miles, as they fly over the Beta region's mountain ranges. They say that young floss who are pure of heart may spot one in the clouds and it's a symbol that they are destined for greatness.

Longfu-yu come in a number of various body types, giving them distinct looks. From the furry hair covered one, to the slick porcelain style, to the metallic armored Longfu. The Longfu-yu are generally harmless, and do not attack Floss who come close to them. They live in isolated places, like mountain tops and inside hidden waterfalls. Many floss seek them out, believing myths that claim they will help guide you if you are worthy. Those who have come in contact with this majestic beast have stated that they are calm, gentle, and elegant.

That said, they are very powerful, and there are rare cases where floss have witnessed a Longfu-yu fighting another petpet! One particular battle shows a Longfu-yu defending a family of floss from a rouge Flogasaurous Rex! Many floss specialists say the Longfu-Yu are somewhat related to the Theta's Anosia Kirin, like a distant cousin, though the Beta refute this claim and tend to not believe in it, since the evidence is sparse at best.

Doubear

2024 . 3rd Wave . Un-Evolved . Levitation

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Doubears!
The Doubears Celebrate their Pet Day on May 2nd
You can claim a free Doubear on that day in our discord server!

The Betan Doubear is a small ground animal that digs into tunnels to burrow. As domesticated pets, they love to cuddle and bury themselves in blankets and pockets. If you have one, it's required to have a warm den for them somewhere in the home to curl up in and feel safe in. The Doubears are docile prey creatures who can be skittish and shy, but when they learn to trust you, they are playful and curious. The Doubears are ideal first pets for young floss. When they're happy, they sometimes use their floating ability in their sleep, causing them to float around the house like a helium balloon.

Kittenfloss love to bop them around and play catch with them until they wake up. Large groups of Doubear in the Betan wild are known to build large and deep tunnels randomly under the brush. It's not uncommon for a Beta child to stumble into one of these huge Doubear holes, either. Legends tell of children who get lost in the tunnels and end up in magical fantasy lands filled with wonder, but most people believe this to be based on a rare occurrence of a Doubear hole leading into another clan. Most children who tumbled down Doubear tunnels can locate a Doubear to aid in escorting them back out by holding onto them as they float to the surface gently. The Doubear don't mind visitors, but they will help you leave when you out stay your welcome.

Doubunni

2026 . 3rd Wave . Evolved . Levitation

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Doubunnis!
The Doubunnis Celebrate their Pet Day on June 21st
You can claim a free Doubunni on that day in our discord server!

The Betan Doubunni is a big, fluffy ball of energy that loves to make itself right at home in forests, plains, and—much to the chagrin of gardeners—your backyard. These professional-grade diggers spend their days engineering deep tunnels, but they are secretly huge suckers for the cozy domestic life. If there's a warm sunbeam or a pile of fresh-out-of-the-dryer laundry nearby, you can bet a Doubunni is already burrowing into it. They are total snuggle bugs that live for blanket forts and movie nights, making them some of the cuddliest roommates a candyfloss could ask for.

Living with a Doubunni is a bit like living with a fuzzy hurricane. These omnivorous critters have a massive amount of pent-up energy and need some serious playtime to keep from getting too rowdy. Their favorite party trick involves using their many wings to launch themselves into the sky. While they can't exactly fly long distances, they use those wings to glide around and boost their jumps up to a whopping twelve feet! Because they love a good view, most owners end up buying giant petpet towers or tall lofts where their Doubunnies can park themselves after a long day of leaping.

If you're looking for a companion that's a bit of a show-off, the Doubunni is definitely your guy. They have a natural talent for levitation, which is why you'll often see them working as the talent in magic shows, popping out of hats and pulling off mid-air flips. They can be a little standoffish and hard to win over at first, but they aren't above a little bribery. If you provide enough tasty snacks and some genuine love, they'll eventually ditch the attitude and become the most loyal, obedient, and playful partner you've ever had.
