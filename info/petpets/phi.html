Mousiki Monkey

2019 . 1st Wave . Pre-Evolved . Duplication

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Mousiki Monkeys!
The Mousiki Monkeys Celebrate their Pet Day on January 27th
You can claim a free Mousiki Monkey on that day in our discord server!

Mousiki monkeys are mischievous and playful. They like to eat anything they can get their hands on and use their multiplication abilities to play tricks on people. These cute mischief-makers are found all over the Phi region and love to gather wherever crowds of Floss are found. Once bonded with a floss they become very protective and loving but are still likely to pick on you once in a while. Get ready for a new dance partner because these energetic buddies love to be active.

Mousiki Kong

2010 . 1st Wave . Evolved . Duplication

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Mousiki Kongs!
The Mousiki Kongs Celebrate their Pet Day on December 21st
You can claim a free Mousiki Kong on that day in our discord server!

They're bigger! They're Faster! They're stronger too! The Mousiki Kongs are bringing down the house this Rythmos! The Mousiki Monkeys can transform into their 2nd form, the Mousiki Kong. These apes are coming to party, and they know how to have a good time!

They stand shoulder high to a Trufflefloss normally but when they rear back on their hind legs they can get up to 8 feet in height. They are top heavy beasts with huge pectoral and upper arm muscles that have been known to rival the strength of a Flogasaurus Rex! When these boys want to party hard, they gather together and beat their palms on the ground making the planet their drums. The Kongs also love to dance! It's said that the art of breakdancing was taught to floss by the Mousiki Kongs!

Candyfloss who take care of Mousiki Kongs know they are not like other petpets! They're loud and stubborn and can be difficult to train so watch out! If your Mousiki Kong wants something they'll let you know! However difficult, training a Mousiki Kong can be very rewarding! These apes are known as the smartest of all petpets and have a ton of personality, and if you befriend one they will be there for you for life! Mousiki Kongs are protective of their owners and with their ability to multiply themselves they are a force to be reckoned with!

Caipeito Gator

2021 . 2nd Wave . Pre-Evolved . Duplication

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Caipeito Gators!
The Caipeito Gators Celebrate their Pet Day on Febuary 26th
You can claim a free Caipeito Gator on that day in our discord server!

Caipeito Gators live high in the jungles of Phi. With their wings they soar above the trees and nest in the lush green canopy. They are semi-aquatic and like to hunt and rest in the cool waters of the Phi rivers.

Caipeito Gators are excellent hunters that usually go after small game and fish. When hunting in the wild they will sit still for a very long time waiting for prey. They gather lots of bugs on them in this way and tropical jawbreakers will take advantage of this for the meal it provides. It is not uncommon to see a flock of Caipeito with jawbreakers mixed in. However, Caipeito are known to get in fights with Mousiki Monkeys and will eat them if the opportunity arises, seeing the two fight with their duplication powers is quite terrifying.

They can be tamed as long as they are properly conditioned from a young age to rely on Candyfloss for food and affection. They like having access to a swimming pool and high shelves to fly up to and sleep. Many owners of the Caipeito Gators set up netting in high corners of their home for them to nest in. Domestic Caipeito are rather lazy and like to flop about on their big claws and snap their jaws for attention. The Phi capitol Mercury City has named the Caipeito Gator as their city mascot and the streets of the city can see countless flocking to the sunniest areas. The city bans any harm to come to the wild Caipeito and people are encouraged to feed or take them into a loving home.

Konfetti Gator

2022 . 2nd Wave . Evolved . Duplication

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Konfetti Gators!
The Konfetti Gators Celebrate their Pet Day on April 21st
You can claim a free Konfetti Gator on that day in our discord server!

The Konfetti Gators are fierce yet surprisingly mellow beasts. They are long and heavy with incredibly thick skin and usually large wings. They sleep high in the trees and like to hunt and rest in the many swamps of the Phi jungle. These lazy lizards are well known for letting prey hang around them and even rest on their bodies. As long as other food is nearby, they will resist gobbling up anyone in their posse. This includes Candyfloss!

Konfetti Gators are a force to be reckoned with when they're angry, but if you're on their good side and keep them fed and happy then they can be very loyal - albeit lazy- pets!
It's said that a resting Konfetti Gator can sit so still that it appears to be dead for hours at a time!

Though their wings are powerful, they tend to not soar too much higher than the tops of the Phi Jungle trees and building tops. They wont go out of their way to hunt prey that is too much trouble unless they're desperate.

These beasts loooooove music! Like most of the jungle dwellers the Konfetti gators love to groove to music and can even be found making their own beats in their family groups or with their Candyfloss Companions. Though they are big enough for a Floss to mount, these lazy boys will flop down and refuse to move if anyone heavier than a kitten floss hops on. The Konfetti Gators mate for life, and tend to hang around their mates and children while their young. A clutch of eggs usually gives 5-7 Caipeito Gators and they are known to lay on each other in a big dog pile under the protective wings of their parents.

Starlotte

2023 . 3rd Wave . Un-Evolved . Duplication

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Starlottes!
The Starlottes Celebrate their Pet Day on March 26th
You can claim a free Starlotte on that day in our discord server!

The Starlotte are tiny, squishy creatures that float about and flock to floss that have extraordinary talent! They chirp in adorable tones that sound like autotuned kitties, and each Starlotte has a particular melody they latch onto and sing frequently throughout their lives. If you place several Starlottes in the same area, they will harmonize and begin to synchronize their personal tunes to make a sick beat.

The heads of the Starlottes are not connected to their bodies though, somehow. They float just above the neck and while things can be passed through the empty space, the head cannot be pulled further from its resting place. It can be spun around however, and it's common for the owners of Starlotte to spin their heads around idly like fidget spinners. The Starlotte don't mind and tend to giggle when their heads are spun.

Synthlotte

2024 . 3rd Wave . Evolved . Duplication

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Synthlottes!
The Synthlottes Celebrate their Pet Day on June 24th
You can claim a free Synthlotte on that day in our discord server!

These little fairies live all over Caramella but originate on the Island of Karpos. They are very musical creatures who will always be humming and singing and harmonizing. They are easy prey for the creatures of the Phi jungles and are extremely docile and stupid. They will gladly sit on the head of a Konfetti Gator and sing a little tune, unaware of the danger they put themselves in. However, despite their bad habits these little tricksters can be hard to catch. They flutter and fly at high speeds and can shoot up into the air very quickly while twirling and giggling.

The sounds that the Synthlotte make sound like autotune, and they often make calls that sound like a theremins. They are known to repeat melodies they hear and sounds that can even sound like lyrics. They can retain the songs they hear for years. Synthlottes make great pets for floss, and they are very chill and easy to care for, as long as you don't mind all the noise they make.

When they are lonely the Synthlotte will duplicate itself to harmonize with its own voice. Though they are docile they can be destructive and prone to running away - they don't mean to, they just get easily distracted and can't find their way home - so when they're not being handled or closely monitored by their floss companions, it's common to keep a Synthlotte in a cage or on a harness. The Synthlottes are egg laying critters, and to protect their eggs from predators they will build nests in tall trees and hollows. In the home, they love to cozy up in their little enclosures and nest in blankets and towels. They eat berries, fruits, and nuts and will take food from wherever they can. City dwelling Synths are known to pick from the garbage, but this is very bad for their health. Some floss will build Synthhouses in their backyards to encourage these singing creatures to nest in their yard and provide them with beautiful music in exchange for a safe home. The Synthlotte and the Kappan Smolvolaki are natural enemies and will go out of their way to fight and kill each other in the wild.

Warning! Never get a Smolvolaki and a Synthlotte in the same room or you will have a madhouse on your hands.
