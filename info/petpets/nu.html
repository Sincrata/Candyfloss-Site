Dyopaw

2020 . 1st Wave . Pre-Evolved . Forcefield

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Dyopaw!
The Dyopaws Celebrate their Pet Day on July 3rd
You can claim a free Dyopaw on that day in our discord server!

Two personalities sharing one body - the Dyopaw are living embodiment of cooperation and unity. Each head of the Dyopaw has an incomplete face, relying on it's better half to survive. Some are born with mismatched faces, which require extra care from their Candyfloss owners.

The Dyopaw are often at odds with themselves, struggling between laziness and hyperactivity, timidness and bravery, obedience and defiance.

Learning to find a common ground is the ultimate goal for these feisty puppies, and with the help of their Candyfloss friends and proper training they can reach peace. Dryopaw are able to produce forcefields and often use them to play and get around. They can be trained to be used as Service Animals for Floss with disabilities or for protection for weak or vulnerable floss. Dyopaws are chubby and small and make perfect lap cuddlers and adventure companions. They love to play and run and do tricks and make great pals for your Kittenfloss. Dont let them near the Charicat though, as these beasts are mortal enemies! If theres one thing both Dyopaw can agree on its the desire to capture a Charicat and foil it's devious intentions. Protectors by nature and lovers at heart the Dyopaw will stop at nothing to please it's Candyfloss family. Dont miss your chance to grab a rambunctious Dyopaw while you're in Nu for the big game!

Tryopaw

2021 . 1st Wave . Evolved . Forcefield

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Tryopaws!
The Tryopaws Celebrate their Pet Day on June 19th
You can claim a free Tryopaw on that day in our discord server!

Stronger! Faster! More heads! These wrinkly beasts are a massive undertaking for any floss who chooses to raise one! The Tryopaw are glorious doggies that are extremely loyal and playful! Their three heads all have unique personalities and require different levels of training and attention from their floss companions! But once they bond with their floss owners, they will love you for life! The Tryopaw is found wild in the hills of Nu and run in packs but are most happy when in the company of Candyfloss!

Standing about twice the height of a Trufflefloss the Dyopaw is the second largest known petpet behind the Flogasaurus Rex! With their strong jaws and sharp claws, they're a force to be reckoned with but they most likely would prefer cuddles and playtime over battle any day! Hard bone plating covers the shared chest of the Tryopaw and some heads lack certain features which leads them to rely on one another for all of their senses.

The Tryopaw can summon strong force fields from their chests and often use them to protect their cubs or their floss family. If you have a Tryopaw be sure to take them often to run through the fields and give them lots of belly rubs.

Macho Almaroo

2022 . 2nd Wave . Pre-Evolved . Forcefield

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Macho Almaroos!
The Macho Almaroos Celebrate their Pet Day on May 30th
You can claim a free Macho Almaroo on that day in our discord server!

The Macho Almaroo call the grasslands of Nu their home. There small marsupials leap and bound with their strong back legs through the brush and grasses that spot the hills. No larger than a Floga Drakai, the Macho Almaroo is a great companion and pet for the floss of Nu.

However they are a bit to handle at first, the Macho Almaroo is a tough opponent, and love to kick, tackle and claw at anything they deem a threat. Even the great Tryopaw stay away from a pack of Almaroo, or they'd walk away with three bloody snouts! Tame Macho Almaroo can even play ball! Incredibly smart, these petpets can be taught to bounce back balls and even kick them into goal nets if they're trained well, making them great buddies to the aspiring Omodaball players of Nu!

Their forcefields are not incredibly strong but they're known to use them as bouncing pads allowing them to take turns at high speeds and jump higher than they should be able to, almost like a double jump!

The pouch on their belly is used to carry small items like food, toys, shiny objects they find in the dust, and even their own babies! They have a tendency to steal food from floss and hide it away so be sure to keep an eye on them! They particularly like metal things so watch your coin purse! Though they can be a hand ful the Macho Almaroo is a great loyal companion and fluffy as can be! Their retractable claws and cuddly nature make them perfect protectors and playmates for floss of all ages.

Greater Almaroo

2023 . 2nd Wave . Evolved . Forcefield

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Greater Almaroos!
The Greater Almaroos Celebrate their Pet Day on October 11th
You can claim a free Greater Almaroo on that day in our discord server!

The Greater Almaroo are tough opponents who love to pick fights with each other, other petpets or even unsuspecting floss that cross their paths. With their insane strength in their punches and kicks the Greater Almaroo is the master of hand-to-hand combat, and to put a cherry on top their mastery of force field generation makes their defenses just as impressive. Their thick, strong upper arms are used for boxing their foes and their powerful back legs allow them to leap and bound like there's no tomorrow.

These powerful creatures tend to travel solo or in small family groups through the grasslands of Nu. They hold their young in the pouch on their bellies, supported by their middle arms, which are much gentler and softer than their other limbs specifically to care for their tender young. When they do not have a cub in their pouch, they often squirrel away things for later and are even known to give rides to other petpets like rainblo or jawbreakers that befriend them. As pets the Greater Almaroo can be a lot to handle, standing just a head under the height of a Sugarfloss and requiring a lot of exercise and proper obedience training they are not pets for the faint of heart.

However, if you are able to properly care for one, they can be great companions and are very loyal. They are even known to allow kittenfloss that they belong to ride in their pouch and are said to be very protective of the kids in their families.

Bala-Bala

2024 . 3rd Wave . Un-Evolved . Forcefield

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Bala-Balas!
The Bala-Balas Celebrate their Pet Day on December 9th
You can claim a free Bala-Bala on that day in our discord server!

The Bala-Bala is a playful Petpet! Thought to be relatives of the Mousiki, these creatures are energetic, curious and affectionate. The Bala-Bala's live in large family groups in the forests of the Nu outback.

They love to swing from the trees, dig in the dirt and run through the arid grasslands that they call home.

These small gymnasts love to use their force shields to create platforms and ramps to help them navigate their environment and play on. It's not uncommon to see a Bala-Bala create a tall jungle-gym style structure for itself and its companions to swing and jump around on for no apparent reason besides from having fun.

The Bala-Bala is highly intelligent when taught by a Candyfloss, and some have even been able to pick up light rudimentary sign language. They tend to create strong bonds with multiple floss and are very trusting. They are great pets for large families. They are omnivorous creatures, but prefer to eat fruit, bugs and small rodents, so be sure to keep them well fed if they share a space with a Jawbreaker Bat! You can even give a ball to a Bala-Bala and it will be entertained for ages. Bala-Bala pet owners are known to have large baskets overflowing with their pets' toys. These rambunctious scamps can be a handful, but their curious, silly, and affectionate nature make them beloved in all of Caramella.

Bala-Mega

2026 . 3rd Wave . Evolved . Forcefield

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Bala-Megas!
The Bala-Megas Celebrate their Pet Day on September 28th
You can claim a free Bala-Mega on that day in our discord server!

The Bala Mega is a large primate home in the arid biomes of the Nu Outback. Standing roughly equal the height of an average popfloss, these highly athletic creatures are characterized by their immense energy and remarkably docile temperament. While they are primarily omnivorous—subsisting on a diet of local fruits and insects—they are opportunistic hunters known to forage for rodents and fish in rare water sources. Their social structure is deeply familial, with parents frequently seen transporting multiple infants who cling securely to their thick fur during transit.

What truly distinguishes the Bala Mega is its adept ability to project solid force fields in midair. These primates utilize this power to construct elaborate architectural feats known as Bala Towers, which serve as both communal living quarters and massive jungle gyms. By manifesting these platforms at high speeds, a pack can bound across the landscape, leaping off force anchors, pipes and platforms to traverse great distances without ever touching the grass. These structures are a testament to their spatial intelligence and need for constant physical engagement.

Beyond their survival tactics, Bala Mega are celebrated for their high intelligence and playfulness. They are easily trained and possess a natural affinity for organized sports, particularly those involving projectile coordination. They show a distinct preference for games that require chasing, throwing, and catching balls, often incorporating their force-field generation to create vertical playing fields. Their ability to bond with candyfloss trainers and their vibrant, spirited nature make them one of the most engaging species in Caramella.
