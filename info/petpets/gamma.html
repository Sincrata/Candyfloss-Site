Petrinos Trogon

2020 . 1st Wave . Pre-Evolved . Super Strength

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Petrinos Trogon!
The Petrinos Trogons Celebrate their Pet Day on October 14th
You can claim a free Petrinos Trogon on that day in our discord server!

The Pétrinos Trógon are a gentle giant, about the size of an earth horse, the Pétrinos Trógon are the one of the largest PetPet to date. The Gammafloss have lived in harmony with the Pétrinos Trógon for as long as they've colonized the area and have learned much from their long-legged companions. Pétrinos Trógon are incredibly strong and can live very long lives because of their resilience. Most Pétrinos Trógon are very gentle and kind to other creatures but young, untrained Pétrinos Trógon often rough house and buck their riders.

The Gamma are the only Candyfloss able to withstand an unruly Pétrinos Trógon's temper tantrums and Floss who are able to hang onto a bucking Pétrinos Trógon are celebrated and compete in the rodeo to see who is the strongest. The Pétrinos Trógon aren't harmed in the Agrios Rontéo and are simple playing rough with the Candyfloss. However, if a Floss is bucked from a Pétrinos Trógon the creature may still want to play and even a Gamma is in danger of an overly excited Pétrinos Trógon when they've just been thrown to their backs, thats where the Rontéo Clowns hop out to distract the playful Pétrinos Trógon until the rider can recover. When it's not Agrios Rontéo time the Pétrinos Trógon can be found running in herds across the vast flatlands of Gamma and helping out at the mines and farms with their massive pulling strength. The Gamma love their Pétrinos Trógon and it wouldn't be the Gamma clan without them.

Vounos Trogon

2021 . 1st Wave . Evolved . Super Strength

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Vounos Trogons!
The Vounos Trogons Celebrate their Pet Day on August 25th
You can claim a free Vounos Trogon on that day in our discord server!

Do you hear that rumbling? The planet shaking beneath your feet? Better run! It's a heard of massive Vouno Trogan!! These huge burly beasts are the evolution of the Gamma's trusty steed, but unlike Petrinos the Vouno are stubborn and very carefree creatures! All the Vouno Trogan want to do is eat and sleep! These huge guys require an incredible amount of food to fuel their strong muscle-covered bodies and what's their favorite food? Rocks of course! But not just any rocks, the Vounos' just LOVE the mountain rocks and boulders from the Gamma's mountain range!

These monsters dominate the wild fields and grasslands at the base of the mountain and are known to dig out entire cave systems underground as they eat and eat! That's why the floss call them the Mountain Eaters! Stubborn as they might be, the Gamma have found their own ways to make use of these huge creatures. If you feed it and give it lots of room even the Vounos will stick around. They can be used for plowing fields and pulling carts weighing tons of pounds. Their strength rival that of the strongest Gammafloss! They also provide delicious creams and milks if they're treated properly! Don't let their lazy eyes fool you, a heard of Vouno Trogan are said to be mightier than a Charion, Paizo beast, and Flogasaurus combined! With their rock hard exterior and sharp horns, the Vouno are a living embodiment of the phrase, " a good defense is the best offense"

Chimecabra

2022 . 2nd Wave . Pre-Evolved . Super Strength

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Chimecabras!
The Chimecabras Celebrate their Pet Day on January 11th
You can claim a free Chimecabra on that day in our discord server!

The Chimacabra are a menace that ravishes the flatlands and farmlands of mid and south Gamma. These vicious snake-dogs are as large as a Paizo dog and as mean as can be. Territorial in nature the Chimacabra hunt alone and at night, taking down entire Vouno Trogon from their flocks with their venomous mouths.

Despite how it looks the brain of the Chimacabra is actually located on the snake-like tail and as such is hard to kill, with its gamma strength this beast can take several bullets and grazes by horns or claws and will hide its soul gem on its tail when it feels threatened. It runs on it's only set of legs with its front hunched down with impeccable speed and agility. They do not make good pets and are often hunted to prevent high populations near the ranches of Gamma. Those who do try to tame one and take it as a companion are seen as "bad asses" who aren't afraid of being bitten by their own pets. They are illegal to own in some clans.

The Chimacabra's front end is made up of soft peach-fur and wrinkles and its back lined with sturdy rock growths. The snake-like tail is smooth and cold when touched, though this is highly unadvised. They are ruthless beasts that are never fully tame and are rarely seen without a snarl. The venom from a Chimacabra is deadly when not treated immediately, but thankfully they usually lack the strength to tear through a Gammafloss' thick skin. It is ill advised for any other floss to come near them.

Chimecayote

2023 . 2nd Wave . Evolved . Super Strength

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Chimecayotes!
The Chimecayotes Celebrate their Pet Day on November 1st
You can claim a free Chimecayote on that day in our discord server!

Calling the Chimecayote a 'pet' is a little misleading. These vicious animals are near impossible to tame and are wild, dangerous beasts. They move in small packs and hunt wild Trojan and Almaroo that go too deep into their territory. They aren't afraid of anything, and lone floss may end up on their dinner menu too if they're not careful.

The Chimecayote are fast with their powerful hindlegs and rock-hard heads they can down whole oak trees with a single headbutt, and that's just one of their heads, the snakes' appendages that grow from their rear can move silently and with shocking speed. Their many jaws can break boulders in two and the venom from one of their bites can kill a Gamma in seconds. The Cayote's Soul Gem is hidden on one of the three snake heads and can make it very risky and difficult to take one down. Strong rock formations form mirroring the creature's skeleton on certain parts of their bodies that can be near impossible to break. Legends say if you cut off the wrong snake's head, then two more will grow in its place.

Though a bit exaggerated, this is actually not baseless. When a snake head that does not contain the Cayote's Soul Gem is severed, in time it will regrow a new working head! And in very rare cases mutations can occur causing two heads to grow on the same neck. This is super uncommon, but likely is the cause of the legend! These critters are dangerous with a capital D and are generally hunted when they come too close to floss civilization. Though over-hunting of them is discouraged. In some Gamma towns, deep in the brush, you can hear the howling of the Cimecayote at night. It's a lonely sound far off on the horizon.

The Cyutoway Calves are playful and energetic bovine who call the plains of Gamma their home! These adorable, pudgy calves are much heavier than they look, weighing in at around 400 lbs. on average! The Gammafloss that raise them don't have any issues with that, however!

The Cyutoway are extremely docile and not easily spooked. They move in large herds and are said to be able to flatten hills into flatland with their strong stomping. The Cyutoways have been trusty companions to the Gamma for generations. These silly calves are known to stand up on their hind hoofs and trot around on two legs on occasion and are able to leap and climb onto high cliffs. They can't stay upright for too long though, or they'll lose their balance and tip over!

Cyutoway Taura

2026 . 3rd Wave . Evolved . Super Strength

Check out this Community Art of Floss and their Cyutoway Tauras!
The Cyutoway Tauras Celebrate their Pet Day on September 2nd
You can claim a free Cyutoway Taura on that day in our discord server!

The Cyutoway Taura is a large, round creature that looks much like a stout cow. It stands about four feet tall at the back and lives in the wide plains and busy farms of the land of Gamma. These animals move in slow packs, their heavy feet making a steady stomping sound as they wander through the grass. Despite their soft, docile nature, they possess massive strength that allows them to push through almost anything in their way.

The most unique feature of the Taura is the long trunk attached to its face. This trunk acts like a powerful vacuum, sucking up grass, leaves, and water to deliver them to a small, plump mouth. Because they are often hungry, they keep their heads tucked low to the ground. They rarely look up to see where they are going, preferring to focus entirely on the scent of their next meal.

This lack of focus makes the Taura a bit of a clumsy giant. If a rock, a tree, or even a parked car is in their path, they do not walk around it. Instead, they simply keep walking forward, head down, pushing the object along with their powerful bodies and tusks. They are not trying to be mean or destructive; they are simply so focused on sniffing for food that they do not realize they have hit anything at all. They only stop if they find the food they were looking for or if something else catches their attention.

On the farms of Gamma, floss value the Taura because they are hearty and very easy to care for. They do not survive well in the wild, but they thrive under the care of farmers. While they are strong enough to carry Candyfloss riders on their broad backs, they do not make very good steeds. Their slow speed and tendency to wander off toward a tasty patch of clover mean that anyone riding a Taura must be prepared for a very long and distracted journey.
