Celebrate the petpets on their special day! For 24 hours you can submit a drawing of your floss with
the designated petpet and their petpet ref, to receive a free pet myo and make them real!
Check out which pets are coming soon so you can be ready!
Submit them in the Discord's Petpet Days thread when their day starts to claim your new buddy!
Grab the Ping role for Petdays in Self Assigned Roles to get notified when a new pet days starts!

Rules

1. To qualify the design must be valid.

We recommend using our editable psd base, available on the petpet's page.

2. Your artwork MUST clearly feature both the floss and pet, and must have clean lines and finished colors with correctly portrayed and required traits.

Shading is optional, and we will let you know if you need to make edits.

3. Your artwork MUST clearly show the floss and pet interacting.

4. You MAY use bases

The artwork and petpet/floss interaction must look cohesive however, and of course credit the basemaker

5. Art Hosted on Site!

If you would like your image to be posted on the site's petpet pages, just submit as normal. If you would like to opt out, let us know when you submit and we will respect your choice.
